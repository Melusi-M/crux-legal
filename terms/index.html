The deal

CRUX scores how hard you trained relative to your own body and lets you compete on effort. The core app is free: recording workouts, your history, your scores, and league play. CRUX Pro (auto-renewing subscription via the App Store) adds extras such as unlimited highlights and deeper analytics.

Our free-tier promise: features that shipped free stay free. Recording, your own history, and access to your own data will never move behind the paywall.

Subscriptions

  • Managed entirely through your App Store account; cancel any time in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.
  • If a free trial is offered, we notify you on-device before it converts to a paid subscription.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple per App Store policy.

Not medical advice

Effort and readiness scores are estimates for training motivation, not medical guidance. Consult a doctor before starting an exercise program, and stop training if you feel unwell. You are responsible for training safely and for your surroundings while recording outdoor workouts.

Fair play

Leagues run on trust plus anti-tamper checks. Falsifying biometric or workout data, abusing bugs to inflate scores, or harassing other athletes may lead to score invalidation or removal from leagues.
