Privacy Policy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Controller: Emmanqubic (Pty) Ltd, South Africa · privacy@emmanqubic.com

CRUX is built local-first: your workouts, routes, and health metrics live on your phone. This policy explains the little we collect, why, and your rights under POPIA (South Africa) and GDPR.

What we collect

We do not collect: your name or email (no account is required — CRUX creates an anonymous ID on first launch), advertising identifiers, or your contacts. CRUX records location only while a workout you started is running — including while your screen is locked or the app is in the background, so a locked phone never truncates your route — and stops the moment you tap Finish. Location is never recorded outside a workout, and routes never leave your phone unless you export or share them yourself.

Health data

Apple Health (HealthKit) data is used solely to compute your effort and readiness scores. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you share it. Highlight cards you export through the share sheet contain your score and an effort-painted route shape — not map tiles or coordinates.

Your rights

  • Delete everything: Profile → Settings → "Delete all my data" wipes every workout, route, token, and setting from your device immediately, and at the same time deletes every synced record (workouts, baseline, league membership, wearable tokens, profile) from our servers and retires the anonymous ID. Email privacy@emmanqubic.com if you want written confirmation.
  • Access / portability / correction: email privacy@emmanqubic.com.

Retention & security

Synced data is retained while you use CRUX and deleted on request. Transport is TLS; server data is stored with row-level security so only you can read your records. Scores are written only by our servers, never by the app, so leaderboards cannot be edited from a device.

Changes

We will not move data collection in a more invasive direction without asking first, in the app, before it happens. Material changes are announced in-app with 30 days' notice.
