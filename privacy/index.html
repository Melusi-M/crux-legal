Privacy Policy

Effective 10 June 2026 · Controller: Emmanqubic (Pty) Ltd, South Africa · privacy@emmanqubic.com

CRUX is built local-first: your workouts, routes, and health metrics live on your phone. This policy explains the little we collect, why, and your rights under POPIA (South Africa) and GDPR.

What we collect

DataWhere it livesWhy
Workout sessions (sport, duration, effort score)On your device; optionally synced to our servers (Supabase) if sync is enabledScoring, history, leagues
GPS routesOn your device. Routes are not uploaded with sync in v1Journey replay
Heart rate, resting HR, HRV, sleep (Apple Health / Oura)Read on-device; raw samples are processed into a score and stored on your deviceEffort scoring and readiness
Age-derived HR baselineOn your device; synced if sync is enabledPersonal effort calibration
Purchase status (CRUX Pro)RevenueCat (our subscription processor)Entitlements; we never see your card details

We do not collect: your name or email (no account is required), advertising identifiers, your contacts, or background location. CRUX records location only while a workout is running and you have the app open, and stops the moment you finish.

Health data

Apple Health (HealthKit) data is used solely to compute your effort and readiness scores. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access.

Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you share it. Highlight cards you export through the share sheet contain your score and an effort-painted route shape — not map tiles or coordinates.

Your rights

  • Delete everything: Profile → Settings → "Delete all my data" wipes every workout, route, token, and setting from your device immediately. If you used sync, email privacy@emmanqubic.com and we delete server copies within 30 days.
  • Access / portability / correction: email privacy@emmanqubic.com.

Retention & security

Synced data is retained while you use CRUX and deleted on request. Transport is TLS; server data is stored with row-level security so only you can read your records.

Changes

We will not move data collection in a more invasive direction without asking first, in the app, before it happens. Material changes are announced in-app with 30 days' notice.
